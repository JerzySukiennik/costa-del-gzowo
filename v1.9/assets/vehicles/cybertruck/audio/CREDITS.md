# Cyber Truck audio credits

All recordings come from freesound.org and are licensed Creative Commons 0 (public domain). Files were trimmed, looped on zero crossings with a crossfade, padded for gapless looping and levelled. The electric drive unit itself is synthesised in code (src/vehicle/cybertruck/ctAudio.js).

- `tyre_loop.mp3`: [817389](https://freesound.org/people/ViveleVent/sounds/817389/) by ViveleVent (tyre noise on a highway)
- `door_open.mp3`: [488057](https://freesound.org/people/BenDrain/sounds/488057/) by BenDrain (car door opening)
- `door_close.mp3`: [345066](https://freesound.org/people/metrostock99/sounds/345066/) by metrostock99 (car door closing from inside)
- `vault_loop.mp3`: [500313](https://freesound.org/people/martin-bn/sounds/500313/) by martin-bn (electric blinds motor, used for the vault cover)
- `compressor_loop.mp3`: [637713](https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/637713/) by kyles (small compressor, used for the air suspension pump)
- `hiss_loop.mp3`: [457294](https://freesound.org/people/brunoboselli/sounds/457294/) by brunoboselli (air pressure release, used for the suspension lowering)
- `hiss_end.mp3`: [131302](https://freesound.org/people/reg7783/sounds/131302/) by reg7783 (short air release, used when the suspension settles)
